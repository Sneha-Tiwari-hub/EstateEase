CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about EstateEase.

How can I explore available properties?

Visit the Properties section to browse available homes. You can search by location and use filters to find suitable properties.

How can I book a property visit?

Select a property, click Book a Visit, enter your details, and complete the demo payment process.

Can I reschedule my property visit?

Yes. Open My Dashboard and select Reschedule under the booking you want to update.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Open My Dashboard and select Cancel Booking under the relevant booking.

How can I save my favourite properties?

Click the heart icon on a property card. Your favourites will appear in My Dashboard.

Are payments processed online?

No. EstateEase currently uses a simulated payment system for demonstration purposes only. No real payment is processed.
